ANGLIČTINA – PROCVIČOVÁNÍ PODLE VYFOCENÉHO TESTU

Doplň odpovědi do mezer ____, případně vyber jednu z možností a–d.
Hvězdička (*) označuje úlohu, ve které je část znění nebo některá možnost
rekonstruovaná podle fotky. V oddílu G je nově sestavený i výchozí text.
Klíč najdeš až na konci souboru.


A. PREPOSITIONS
Doplň do každé mezery jednu předložku.

1. My wife's career is very important ____ her.
2. A certain amount of flexibility is built ____ the system.
3. I had a strong desire to help and care ____ people.
4. I'd like to help ____ some way, but I'm not sure how.
5. Rivers also provide a means ____ disposing of waste, and act as a transport medium.
6. Obesity is associated ____ vascular disease, diabetes and other grave health problems.
7. Let's start ____ reviewing what we did last week.
8. Many economists are critical ____ the government's economic policies.
9. Stress has an effect ____ both your physical and mental health.
10. Alcohol contributes ____ 100,000 deaths a year in the US.

Každou položku použij nejvýše jednou. GO je v nabídce dvakrát.
Dvě slovesa zbudou.

BE / CLEAN / COME / GO / GO / KEEP / LIVE / OPEN / PUT / STAND / STAY / USE

9. He managed ____ the door without the key.
10. Stand up for something, even if it means ____ alone sometimes.


C. COUNTABLE AND UNCOUNTABLE NOUNS / QUANTIFIERS
Vyber možnost, která nejlépe doplní větu. Chybné tvary v možnostech
jsou součástí zadání.

1. There is ____ on which computer to buy.
   a) advices   b) an advise   c) some advice   d) some advices
2. We're having ____ of trouble with the new computer system.
   a) plenty   b) a lot   c) a few   d) little
3. I've got too many ____ to do; I don't know where to start.
   a) thing   b) things   c) stuff   d) stuffs
4. They have ____ money in the bank.
   a) many of   b) plenty   c) so much   d) a few
5. You can't watch TV until you've finished ____.
   a) your homework   b) your homeworks   c) all your homeworks   d) your the homework

   a) a construction   b) construction   c) many constructions   d) a lot of constructions
7. We haven't got ____ oranges at the moment.
   a) some   b) any   c) much   d) no
8. There was ____ food in the fridge. It was nearly empty.

9. For more ____ about registering for online courses, please contact the registrar's office.
   a) information   b) informations   c) of information   d) of informations
10. ____ get the nutrition from the soil in which they grow.
    a) A plant   b) The plant   c) Plant   d) Plants
11. We are always grateful to receive ____ from our listeners.
    a) feedback   b) feedbacks   c) a feedback   d) some feedbacks

E. ARTICLES *
Doplň a, an, the nebo – (bez členu). Pět vět je kvůli rozmazané fotce
rekonstruováno. Poslední věta je čitelná téměř celá.

1. Can you play ____ piano? There is ____ piano in ____ living room.
2. My sister is ____ teacher. She goes to ____ work by ____ train.
3. She's thinking about studying at ____ university in ____ United States.
4. He was sent to ____ prison for robbing ____ bank.
5. I don't believe I broke my ____ grandmother's vase.


F. KEY WORD TRANSFORMATIONS
Doplň druhou větu tak, aby měla stejný význam jako první.
Použij zadané slovo beze změny. Do mezery patří dvě až pět slov.

1.* The computer was far too expensive for me to buy. (NOT)
    I ____ money to buy the computer.
2.* I found the glasses by chance when I was tidying up my room. (ACROSS)
    I ____ the glasses when I was tidying up my room.
3. Our meal was so great we had to leave a tip. (SUCH)
   It was ____ we had to leave a tip.
4. He is a fun person to play tennis with. (ENJOY)
   I really ____ with him.
5. Did you succeed in contacting the boss today? (MANAGE)
   Did you ____ the boss today?
6. I'm sure it was Tom who created the whole thing. (TOGETHER)
   It must have been Tom who ____.
7. “I won't pay for the damage,” Chris said. (REFUSED)
   Chris ____ for the damage.
8. I wouldn't bother to wash the car because it's going to rain. (WORTH)
   It ____ the car because it's going to rain.
9. My grandfather is one of the last people alive who fought in the war. (VERY)
   Other than my grandfather there ____ who fought in the war.
10. I wish I had not spent so much money last night. (REGRET)
    I ____ so much money last night.
11. He is responsible for looking after his sick mother. (OF)
    He has to ____ his sick mother.
12. She joined the company in 2000 and has developed considerable experience in marketing. (UP)
    She joined the company in 2000 and has ____ considerable experience in marketing.


G. READING COMPREHENSION *
Na fotce jsou vidět útržky o publiku, světle a krabici bonbonů a otázka
na význam slova „peevishly“. Souvislý původní text ani všechny možnosti
nejdou spolehlivě přečíst. Tento náhradní text s otázkami je určený
k samostatnému procvičení stejného typu úlohy.

When the performer lifted the lid of the box of chocolates, two bright
eyes looked out at the audience. The children in the front row laughed.
A man at the back complained peevishly that all he could see was a box.

After the show, I asked to see the trick again. This time I stood beside
the table. From there I could see a small light behind the box. When the
performer moved his hand, the light went out and the eyes disappeared.
I walked back to the front row. Even though I now knew where the light
was, the box still looked perfectly ordinary from my seat.

1. What did the audience see when the performer opened the box?
   a) A hand.   b) Two bright eyes.   c) A mirror.   d) A candle.
2. Why did the man at the back complain?
   a) He could only see the box.   b) Someone had taken his seat.
   c) The children were too loud.   d) The show was over.
3. Which word is closest in meaning to “peevishly”?
   a) irritably   b) proudly   c) eagerly   d) calmly
4. Where was the narrator standing when the light became visible?
   a) Outside the room.   b) Beside the table.
   c) In the front row.   d) Behind the audience.
5. What does the final sentence suggest about the trick?
   a) The box had been replaced.   b) The narrator had forgotten the secret.
   c) The viewer's position mattered.   d) The performer needed a new light.

A. 1 to | 2 into | 3 for | 4 in | 5 of | 6 with | 7 by | 8 of | 9 on | 10 to
   U 7 odpovídá „by“ opravě na papíře. „Start with reviewing“ je také
   možné, i když přirozenější bývá „start by reviewing“.

B. 1 going | 2 to go | 3 to clean | 4 coming | 5 staying | 6 not to be
   7 living | 8 using | 9 to open | 10 standing
   Zbývá KEEP a PUT. Po „helped his flat-mate“ lze říct i „clean“.
   U 8 „tried using“ znamená, že dotyčný náplasti vyzkoušel jako metodu.

C. 1 c | 2 a/b | 3 b | 4 c | 5 a | 6 b | 7 b | 8 a | 9 a | 10 d
   11 a | 12 c | 13 a/c | 14 a | 15 d
   U 2 funguje „plenty of trouble“ i „a lot of trouble“.
   U 13 je běžná neutrální odpověď „a few slices“ (několik krajíců).
   „Few slices“ je také gramaticky možné a zdůrazňuje, že jich bylo málo.

   9 optional | 10 proof
